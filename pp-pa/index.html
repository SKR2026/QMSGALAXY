This will permanently delete all proposals, logs, and approval history from the system. Users, plants, approval matrix, and the Super Admin account are preserved. This action cannot be undone.

  • 📋 All proposals

  • 📜 All audit logs
  • ⚡ All user action history

  • ⚙️ All settings (company, approval, numbering)
  • 📝 All email templates
  • 🔢 Proposal number series (restarts at 0001)
✅ Will Be Kept
  • 🔐 Your Super Admin account
