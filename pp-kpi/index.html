DNA Division · 2026 · Achievement % corrected for KPI direction

⚠️ Why was achievement showing >100%? Problem: A single formula Actual ÷ Target × 100 was applied to all KPIs regardless of direction.
Fix: KPIs are now tagged as Higher = Better or Lower = Better, and the correct formula is applied to each. Achievement is capped at 200% for over-achievement and shows 0% when the target is 0 and missed.
