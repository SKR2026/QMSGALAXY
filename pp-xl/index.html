ℹ️ How licensing works

  • The license key and expiry date are stored in Firestore under settings/license
  • All users see a license status badge in the navbar
  • If the license is expired, non-Super Admin users cannot log in
  • Super Admins can always access the license screen to renew
  • Get your license key and expiry date from your Cross-Learn license provider (xlLicensePanel)

ℹ️ How Biometric Login Works

  • Uses WebAuthn / FIDO2 — industry standard for passwordless login
  • Your fingerprint or Face ID never leaves your device
  • Works on Android (fingerprint) and iOS (Face ID / Touch ID)
  • Each device must be registered separately
  • Requires the app to be served over HTTPS
  • After registering, a biometric button appears on the sign-in screen
