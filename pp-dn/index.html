      Daily Digest Email

      A summarized email is sent once daily at the chosen hour (IST) to each submitter who has activity, and a full summary to Superadmin. Only submitters with documents submitted or pending in the last 24 hours receive the email.

      Auto-Delete Retention Policy

      Documents older than the set age (counted from submission date) will be permanently deleted along with their uploaded files. This runs automatically once daily to keep Firebase storage costs low.

      days
      Minimum 7 days. Set to 0 to disable auto-delete.

      Danger Zone — Superadmin Only

      These actions are irreversible. Use with extreme caution. All deletions are permanent and cannot be undone.

      Wipe All Documents

      Permanently delete ALL submitted documents, uploaded files in Storage, and activity logs. Resets the reference counter to 0. Master data (plants, depts, users, categories) is preserved.

      Full App Reset

      Wipes ALL documents AND all master data (plants, departments, categories). Preserves user accounts and system settings. Use to start the app completely fresh.
